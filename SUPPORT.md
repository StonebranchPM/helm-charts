# Support

## Status

These charts are maintained by the Stonebranch Product & Solutions Management Team
as a **community / internal tooling** project. They are not an official Stonebranch
product release and carry no commercial support SLA.

What this means in practice:
- Issues are addressed on a best-effort basis.
- Breaking changes will be communicated via the `CHANGELOG` and
  [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md).
- The charts target Stonebranch UAC 7.9.x. Compatibility with other versions is
  not tested.

## Getting help

- **Bug reports / feature requests:** open an issue using the templates in
  [`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/).
- **Questions about chart usage:** open a GitHub Discussion (if enabled) or a
  plain issue tagged `question`.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do **not** open a public issue. Contact the
  maintainer directly at len-maurice.seemann@stonebranch.com.
